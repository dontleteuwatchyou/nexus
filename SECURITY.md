# Security & Responsible Use

## Authorisation

Nexus includes active probing (PENTEST) and wrappers around offensive
third-party tools (EXTERNAL). Use them **only** against systems you own or for
which you hold explicit written authorisation (a signed engagement/scope, a lab
you control, or a sanctioned CTF). Unauthorised scanning or attacking of
systems is illegal in most jurisdictions. You are solely responsible for how
you use this software.

Do **not** commit real target lists or scan reports to any repository — see
`.gitignore`. They describe real third-party systems and must stay private.

## Reporting a vulnerability in Nexus itself

Please open a private report (GitHub Security Advisory) or contact the
maintainers rather than filing a public issue. We aim to acknowledge reports
within a few days.
